Add to Home Screen

Install Mac Stories like an app — works offline, no App Store needed.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  • 1Tap the menu button at the left end of Safari's address bar (the icon with small lines). On some phones it's ⋯ three dots in the bottom corner instead.
  • 2Tap Share.
  • 3Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • 4Leave Open as Web App turned on, then tap Add.
On Android (Chrome)
  • 1Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  • 2Tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app" if shown).
  • 3Confirm by tapping "Add" — the app icon will appear on your home screen.
